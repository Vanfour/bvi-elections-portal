Candidate Requirements

Everything you need to know about eligibility, nomination, and documentation required to stand for election in the British Virgin Islands.

person_check

Eligibility Criteria

  • check_circleMust be a British Virgin Islands citizen aged 18 or older
  • check_circleMust be registered as a voter in the district you wish to represent
  • check_circleMust be resident in the BVI for at least 5 continuous years
  • check_circleMust not hold any disqualifying public office
  • check_circleMust not have been convicted of a serious criminal offence
edit_document

Nomination Steps

  1. 1Obtain a Nomination Form from the Elections Office or download below
  2. 2Collect at least 10 signatures from registered voters in your district
  3. 3Pay the nomination deposit fee of $500 USD
  4. 4Submit all documents to the Returning Officer by the deadline
  5. 5Attend Nomination Day for official confirmation
folder_open

Required Documents

  • descriptionCompleted Nomination Form (signed by 10+ witnesses)
  • descriptionValid BVI Passport or National ID
  • descriptionProof of BVI residency (utility bill, lease agreement)
  • descriptionDeclaration of Assets form
  • descriptionPolice clearance certificate (issued within 3 months)
warning

Disqualification Notice

Persons who hold a paid government position, are undischarged bankrupts, or have been convicted of a corrupt or illegal election practice within the past 7 years are ineligible to stand as candidates. Contact the Elections Office for full disqualification criteria.
