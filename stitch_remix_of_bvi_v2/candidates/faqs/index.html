Common Questions

Find answers to the most common questions regarding candidacy and the electoral process.

search

Registration

How do I become a candidate? expand_more
To become a candidate, you must ensure you meet all eligibility requirements, register during the official window, and successfully complete the nomination process on Nomination Day. Detailed guides are available in the 'Forms & Guides' section of our website.
When does candidate registration open? expand_more
Official registration windows are announced by the Supervisor of Elections in the lead-up to an election. Please refer to the 'Important Dates' tab for the specific timeline of the current election cycle.
What documents are required? expand_more
Required documents typically include a valid passport, proof of VI citizenship, statutory declarations of eligibility, and the completed nomination form signed by registered voters in your district.

Registration

How do I submit my nomination? expand_more
Nominations must be submitted in person at the designated Returning Office for your district on Nomination Day between the hours of 10:00 AM and 1:00 PM, or as otherwise gazetted.
Can I edit my nomination after submission? expand_more
Minor corrections can sometimes be made while the Returning Officer is present during the nomination window. Once the window closes and the certificate is issued, no further changes are permitted.
What happens after nomination submission? expand_more
After submission, the Returning Officer will verify your documents and deposit. If everything is in order, you will be formally certified as a candidate for the election.

Registration

Who is eligible to run for election? expand_more
Eligibility is defined by the Virgin Islands Constitution Order and the Elections Act. Generally, you must be a Virgin Islander aged 21 or over and registered as a voter.
Are there any restrictions for candidates? expand_more
Restrictions apply to individuals holding certain public offices, those with certain criminal convictions, or those who are undischarged bankrupts. Consult the Elections Act for a full list of disqualifications.

Registration

What are the campaign rules? expand_more
Campaigns must adhere to the Code of Conduct, which prohibits bribery, intimidation, and the use of public resources for partisan purposes. All advertising must be clearly labeled.
Are there spending limits? expand_more
Yes, candidates and parties must adhere to expenditure limits as defined by the most recent electoral regulations. Financial reports must be submitted following the election.
What activities are allowed during campaign period? expand_more
Allowed activities include public meetings, door-to-door canvassing, media appearances, and the distribution of literature. All activities must cease 24 hours before polling begins.
