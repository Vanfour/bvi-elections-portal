checklist Eligibility Requirements

To stand as a candidate in a BVI General Election, you must meet all of the following criteria under the Elections Act.

check_circle

Belonger Status

Must hold BVI Belonger status as defined in the Constitution.

check_circle

18 Years or Older

Must be at least 18 years of age on Nomination Day.

check_circle

Registered Voter

Must be registered on the current electoral roll in the BVI.

check_circle

Domiciled in BVI

Must be ordinarily resident and domiciled in the Virgin Islands.

check_circle

No Disqualifying Offence

Must not have been convicted of a disqualifying electoral offence.

check_circle

Not a Public Officer

Serving public officers must resign before standing for election.

timeline The Candidacy Process

1

Confirm Your Eligibility

Review the candidate requirements and ensure you meet all legal criteria under the Elections Act before proceeding.

2

Obtain Nomination Papers

Collect your official nomination papers from the Office of the Supervisor of Elections or download from the Forms & Documents section.

3

Secure Nominators

Your nomination must be signed by registered voters in the district you wish to represent. A proposer and seconder are required.

4

Pay the Nomination Deposit

A nomination deposit of $500 USD is required. This is refundable if you receive more than one-eighth of the valid votes cast.

5

Submit on Nomination Day

Deliver your completed nomination papers and deposit to the Returning Officer on or before Nomination Day, October 25, 2024.
