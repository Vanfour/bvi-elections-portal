Key Dates

Stay informed with the electoral calendar to ensure you don't miss any critical deadlines.

Nomination Day

Prospective candidates must submit their nomination papers and deposits at designated returning offices across the Territory.

OCT 15

Campaign Period

Official window for candidate promotion and public engagement following the certification of nominees.

OCT 16 - NOV 05

Election Day

Territory-wide general election polls open from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

NOV 25

Result Certification

Official publication of final vote tallies and the gazetting of elected representatives by the Supervisor of Elections.
