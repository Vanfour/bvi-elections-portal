Become a Poll Worker

Serve your community and play a vital role in ensuring free and fair elections.

Why Become a Poll Worker?

Poll workers are the backbone of every election. They ensure that every eligible voter can cast their ballot fairly, securely, and with dignity.

volunteer_activism

Serve Your Community

Make a direct impact on democracy in the BVI.

payments

Receive Compensation

Poll workers receive a stipend for their service.

school

Free Training Provided

Full training before Election Day at no cost to you.

checklist Eligibility Requirements

check_circle

BVI Citizen or Belonger

Must hold BVI status or belonger status.

check_circle

18 Years or Older

Must be of legal age on Election Day.

check_circle

Registered Voter

Must be on the current electoral roll.

check_circle

Not a Candidate

Cannot be standing for election.

check_circle

Available on Election Day

Must be available from 5:00 AM to 8:00 PM on November 25, 2024.

check_circle

Attend Training

Must complete mandatory pre-election training sessions.

badge Available Roles

manage_accounts

Presiding Officer

Responsible for the overall administration of the polling station. Manages staff and ensures procedures are followed.

how_to_vote

Poll Clerk

Verifies voter identity against the electoral roll and issues ballot papers to eligible voters.

security

Ballot Box Officer

Oversees the secure handling of ballot boxes throughout the day, including sealing at close of polls.

accessibility_new

Voter Assistance Officer

Assists elderly, disabled, or otherwise impaired voters in exercising their right to vote.

timeline What to Expect

1

Submit Your Application

Complete the application form on this page. Applications close on October 31, 2024.

2

Receive Your Appointment Letter

Successful applicants will receive a formal appointment letter by email within 2 weeks of applying.

3

Attend Mandatory Training

All poll workers must attend a training session the week before Election Day. Location and time will be in your appointment letter.

4

Serve on Election Day

Report to your assigned polling station by 5:00 AM on November 25, 2024. Polls close at 6:00 PM.
