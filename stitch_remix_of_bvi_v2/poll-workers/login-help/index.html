Login Help

Trouble signing in? We'll get you sorted.

Common Login Issues

I didn't receive my login credentials expand_more

Your Worker ID and temporary password are sent to the email address you provided during registration. Please check:

  • check_circleYour spam or junk folder
  • check_circleThat you're checking the correct email account
  • check_circleEmails from noreply@elections.gov.vg

If you still can't find it, contact the Elections Office to have your credentials resent.

I forgot my password expand_more

To reset your password, contact the Elections Office with your full name and Worker ID. A new temporary password will be sent to your registered email within one business day.

For urgent requests on or close to Election Day, call us directly at +1 (284) 468-3701.

My Worker ID is not recognised expand_more

Worker IDs follow the format PW-YYYY-XXXX (e.g. PW-2024-0042). Make sure you are entering it exactly as shown in your appointment letter, including the dashes.

If your ID still isn't recognised, your account may not yet be activated. Please allow 24 hours after receiving your appointment letter, then contact us if the issue persists.

My account has been locked expand_more

Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts. Please contact the Elections Office to unlock your account. For security purposes, we cannot unlock accounts via email — you will need to call or visit in person with a valid photo ID.

The portal is not loading or showing an error expand_more

Please try the following steps:

  • check_circleClear your browser cache and cookies
  • check_circleTry a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
  • check_circleDisable browser extensions
  • check_circleCheck your internet connection

If the issue persists, contact our technical support team.
