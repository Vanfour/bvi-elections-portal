Important Update for Candidates: Nomination Submission Process

person

Department of Elections

Official Communications Office

Link copied!

The Department of Elections wishes to notify all prospective candidates for the 2024 BVI General Election of important changes to the nomination submission process. These changes take immediate effect and apply to all candidates contesting seats in any of the nine districts or the at-large category.

Key Changes to Nomination Submissions

Following a review of the nomination process, the following amendments have been made to ensure greater accuracy and transparency in the filing of nomination papers:

  • All nomination papers must now be submitted in person at the designated Returning Officer's office for the relevant district
  • Electronic or postal submissions will no longer be accepted for primary nomination documents
  • A non-refundable nomination deposit of $500 (district) or $1,000 (at-large) is required at time of submission
  • Two qualified nominators who are registered voters in the relevant district must be present at submission
  • All supporting documents must be certified copies, not originals

Nomination Deadline

The deadline for the submission of all nomination papers remains Nomination Day, October 29, 2024. Returning Officers will be available at their respective offices from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM on Nomination Day.

Candidates are strongly advised to submit their papers well in advance of the deadline to allow time for any queries or corrections to be resolved before the close of nominations.

Required Documents Checklist

  • Completed Nomination Form (Form C) — available from the Elections Office
  • Certified copy of valid BVI Passport or National Identity Card
  • Proof of BVI Status or Belonger status
  • Signed declarations from two nominators registered in the district
  • Nomination deposit (cash or bank draft payable to the Government of the Virgin Islands)
  • Completed Campaign Finance Declaration form

Contact Your Returning Officer

Each district has a designated Returning Officer who can answer questions about the nomination process. Contact details for all Returning Officers are available on the Elections Office website or by calling the main Elections Office line.

warning

Important Notice

Incomplete or incorrectly filed nomination papers will be rejected. The Department of Elections cannot accept late submissions under any circumstances. Candidates are encouraged to seek legal advice if uncertain about any requirements.
