New Voter Guidelines Released for 2024 General Election

person

Department of Elections

Official Communications Office

Link copied!

The Department of Elections of the British Virgin Islands is pleased to announce the release of updated voter guidelines ahead of the November 25, 2024 General Election. These updated procedures have been developed in consultation with electoral stakeholders to ensure a smooth, transparent, and accessible voting experience for all eligible citizens.

What Has Changed

The revised guidelines introduce several key updates to the voter identification and polling station procedures. All voters are now required to present a valid government-issued photo ID at their assigned polling station before receiving a ballot paper.

  • Valid BVI Passport or National Identity Card now required at the polls
  • Voter registration cards alone are no longer sufficient as primary ID
  • Advance poll application window extended to 14 days before Election Day
  • New accessible voting booths installed at all nine polling stations
  • Voter assistance officers available at every station to support those in need

Voter Identification Requirements

To cast a ballot in the 2024 General Election, each voter must present one of the following forms of photo identification: a valid BVI Passport, a BVI Driver's Licence, or an official National Identity Card issued by the Government of the Virgin Islands.

Voters who do not possess any of the above documents are encouraged to contact the Elections Office no later than November 15, 2024 to discuss alternative verification arrangements.

Polling Station Locations

All nine district polling stations remain at their previously designated locations. A full list of stations, along with accessibility information and opening hours, is available on the Elections Office website and at all government offices across the Territory.

  • Polls open at 6:00 AM and close at 6:00 PM on November 25, 2024
  • Voters in the queue before 6:00 PM will be permitted to vote
  • No campaigning is permitted within 200 metres of any polling station

Further Information

For the full text of the updated voter guidelines, please download the PDF document below or visit any of the Department of Elections' public offices. Members of the public may also contact the Elections Office by phone or email with any questions.
