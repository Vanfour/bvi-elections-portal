Changes in Election Schedule: Revised Timeline for Key Electoral Events

person

Department of Elections

Official Communications Office

Link copied!

The Department of Elections of the British Virgin Islands hereby notifies all candidates, voters, poll workers, and members of the public of revisions to the electoral calendar for the 2024 General Election. These changes have been necessitated by administrative requirements and have been approved by the Supervisor of Elections.

Revised Key Dates

The following dates have been updated from the originally published electoral calendar. All other dates remain unchanged. Please note these revisions and update your records accordingly:

EventOriginal DateRevised Date
Voter Registration DeadlineNov 1, 2024Nov 8, 2024
Nomination DayOct 29, 2024Oct 29, 2024 (unchanged)
Poll Worker TrainingNov 15, 2024Nov 18, 2024
Advance PollNov 21–22, 2024Nov 21–22, 2024 (unchanged)
Election DayNov 25, 2024Nov 25, 2024 (unchanged)

Reason for Changes

The extension of the Voter Registration Deadline from November 1 to November 8, 2024 has been granted to allow additional time for citizens who have recently turned 18, or who have relocated within the Territory, to update their registration details on the Sovereign Ledger.

The Poll Worker Training date has been moved from November 15 to November 18 to ensure that all training materials incorporating the latest procedural updates are available. All appointed poll workers will be notified of their confirmed training venue by November 14, 2024.

What You Need to Do

  • Voters: Ensure your registration details are updated before November 8, 2024
  • Candidates: No action required — nomination dates remain unchanged
  • Poll Workers: Note the new training date of November 18, 2024
  • All parties: Download the updated electoral calendar below
