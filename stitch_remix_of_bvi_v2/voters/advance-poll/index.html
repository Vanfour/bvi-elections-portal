Apply for Advance Poll

Can't make it on Polling Day? Apply to cast your vote early at a designated advance polling station.

info

Advance Poll Dates: November 21–22, 2024

Advance polling is available for registered voters who are unable to vote on Election Day (November 25). You must apply in advance to use this service.

edit_document Advance Poll Application

Complete this form to register for advance polling. Applications must be submitted by November 15, 2024.

check_circle

Application Submitted!

You will receive a confirmation reference number by email within 2 business days.

person_check Who Can Apply?

  • work Working on Polling Day and unable to leave during polling hours
  • flight Absent from BVI on Polling Day due to work or study
  • medical_services Have a medical condition or disability preventing attendance
  • church Have a religious observance on Polling Day
  • family_restroom Are a caregiver unable to leave dependants
