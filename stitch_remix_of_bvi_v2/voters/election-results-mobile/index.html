Top Questions

Am I eligible to vote in the Virgin Islands? expand_more
To be eligible, you must be a Belonger aged 18 or older and be domiciled in the Virgin Islands, or satisfy specific residency requirements as defined by the Elections Act.
How do I register to vote for the first time? expand_more
You can register by visiting the Office of the Supervisor of Elections with your original Belonger card or passport.
What documents are needed for registration? expand_more
You will need your original Virgin Islands Passport or Belonger's Card. Additional proof of address may be required.
How can I check my registration status? expand_more
You can check your status online using our 'Check My Status' tool above, or by calling the Office of the Supervisor of Elections.
