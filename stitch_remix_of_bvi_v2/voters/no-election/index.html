Am I eligible to vote in the Virgin Islands? expand_more
To be eligible, you must be a Belonger aged 18 or older and be domiciled in the Virgin Islands, or satisfy specific residency requirements as defined by the Elections Act.
How do I register to vote for the first time? expand_more
You can register by visiting the Office of the Supervisor of Elections with your original Belonger card or passport. You will need to complete the Voter Registration Form.
What documents are needed for registration? expand_more
You will need your original Virgin Islands Passport or Belonger's Card. Additional proof of address may be required in some instances.
How can I check my current registration status? expand_more
You can check your status online using our 'Check My Status' tool in the Key Actions area, or by calling the Office of the Supervisor of Elections.
What are the polling station opening times? expand_more
On Election Day, all polling stations throughout the Virgin Islands are officially open from 6:00 AM until 6:00 PM.
