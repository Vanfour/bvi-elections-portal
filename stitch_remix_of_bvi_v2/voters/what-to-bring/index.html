Election Day — November 25, 2024

What to Bring

Everything you need to bring to your polling station on Election Day to cast your vote successfully.

location_on Find My Polling Station
how_to_vote Polls are open today — 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Monday, November 25, 2024
priority_high

Required — You Must Bring

You will not be permitted to vote without one of the following valid photo identification documents.

badge

Virgin Islands Belonger's Card

Primary ID

Your original Virgin Islands Belonger's Card is the primary accepted form of identification at the polling station. Must be valid and not expired.

travel_explore

Virgin Islands Passport

Primary ID

A valid Virgin Islands Passport is accepted as proof of identity. Your passport must be valid — expired passports will not be accepted.

check

Recommended to Bring

These items are not mandatory but will help the process go smoothly and quickly.

how_to_reg

Voter Registration Card

If you received a voter registration card, bring it along. It confirms your polling station assignment and speeds up the verification process.

location_on

Your Polling Station Address

Know your assigned polling station before you leave home. Use the "Find My Polling Place" tool to confirm your location in advance.

Find My Polling Place arrow_forward
wb_sunny

Water & Sun Protection

Polling stations may have queues, especially in the early morning and late afternoon. Bring water and wear sunscreen if you expect to wait outdoors.

block

Do Not Bring

cancel

Campaign Materials

Do not wear or carry any campaign materials, party paraphernalia, or candidate insignia inside the polling station.

cancel

Recording Devices in the Booth

Photography or video recording inside the polling booth is strictly prohibited. Ballots must remain private.

cancel

Additional Persons in the Booth

Only the voter is allowed in the booth at one time. Exceptions apply for voters who require assistance — speak to the Presiding Officer.
